Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

PhysioDeutsch ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Berkin Ayberk, an individual based in Austria. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the PhysioDeutsch mobile app (the "App"), why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

Because we are based in Austria, we process personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), regardless of where you are located.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Data controller: Berkin Ayberk
Contact: hello@physiodeutsch.app

2. What data we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Name (as you enter it at sign-up), email address, password (stored encrypted)To create and secure your account and to address you in the App
Learning progress (learned words and phrases, bookmarks, XP, streaks, quiz scores, review schedule)To save your progress, sync it across your devices and show you statistics
App preferences (interface language, dark mode, sound effects)To remember your settings. These are stored on your device only.
Subscription status (free / premium)To unlock the features you've paid for

We do not collect your phone number, precise location, or payment card details.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide and operate the App (account login, progress sync across devices)
  • To respond to support requests you send us
  • To process Premium subscription purchases (see Section 4)
  • To improve the App, using aggregated, anonymized usage patterns

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

These providers may process data outside the EU/EEA (e.g. in the United States). Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

If we introduce analytics or crash-reporting tools in the future (e.g. to see which lessons are most used), we will update this policy before doing so, and any such data will be anonymized or pseudonymized wherever possible.

5. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

  • Contract — processing needed to provide the App and your account (Art. 6(1)(b))
  • Legitimate interest — improving and securing the App (Art. 6(1)(f))
  • Consent — where we ask for it separately, e.g. marketing emails, if introduced (Art. 6(1)(a))

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the App under Settings → Delete Account; this immediately deletes your account, profile and learning progress from our database. Any remaining copies (for example in backups) are deleted within 30 days, except where we are required to keep data longer by law.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — in Austria, the Datenschutzbehörde

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@physiodeutsch.app. You can also delete your account and data directly in the App under Settings → Delete Account.

8. Children

PhysioDeutsch is intended for physiotherapy professionals and students, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes will be communicated in-app.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hello@physiodeutsch.app.
